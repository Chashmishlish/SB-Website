INTRODUCTION OF

Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi

CEO'S GRANDFATHER (LATE)

Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi was a pioneering force in Karachi’s real estate landscape and a visionary entrepreneur whose legacy continues to inspire generations.

In 1982, he founded M.A. Estate with a commitment to honesty, transparency, and exceptional client service. At a time when the real estate sector was rapidly evolving, he established new standards of professionalism, integrity, and trust, earning the confidence of investors, property owners, and developers across Karachi.

Through his tireless dedication, strong leadership, and deep understanding of the property market, M.A. Estate grew into one of the city’s most respected real estate firms. His unwavering principles and outstanding reputation earned him the prestigious position of Chairman of the All Karachi Property Dealers Association, where he worked tirelessly to unite the real estate community, promote ethical business practices, resolve industry challenges, and strengthen cooperation among property professionals.

Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi was widely admired not only for his business achievements but also for his humility, generosity, and commitment to helping others. His guidance and visionary approach left a lasting impact on Karachi’s real estate industry, influencing countless professionals and setting a benchmark for future generations.

Although he passed away in 1994, his values, principles, and entrepreneurial spirit continued to inspire those who followed in his footsteps. Carrying forward his vision and commitment to excellence, his son, Shahzad Qureshi, established Shahzad Builders & Developers, a construction and real estate development company founded on the same principles of integrity, quality, and trust. Under his leadership, the company has continued the family’s legacy by delivering modern residential and commercial developments, further strengthening the reputation built by Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi and contributing to the growth of Pakistan’s real estate and construction industry.

Today, the legacy of Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi lives on through Shahzad Builders & Developers, reflecting a continuous journey of excellence, innovation, and unwavering dedication to serving clients and shaping the future of real estate.
