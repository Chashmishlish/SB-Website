INTRODUCTION OF

Rizwan Qureshi

MANAGING DIRECTOR (MD)

The Managing Director, Rizwan Qureshi, began his journey alongside the CEO in the early stages of Shahzad Builders & Developers. From the beginning, he stood beside him with a shared vision and took on the responsibility of turning ideas into action, helping shape the company’s strong foundation and steady growth.

He looks after the company’s overall operations with a clear focus on planning, coordination, and timely execution. His role includes project development, business strategy, financial planning, resource management, and risk evaluation — ensuring each initiative is handled with care and delivers the right results.

Known for his practical approach and sound judgment, he converts ideas into workable plans and follows through with discipline. He values clear communication, strong systems, and empowered teams, which help the organization function smoothly and adapt with confidence.

Beyond projects, he works on strengthening internal processes, improving efficiency, and building a culture of accountability. His belief in long-term relationships, ethical practices, and continuous improvement shapes the way the company operates.

His association with the organization is built on alignment of purpose and mutual respect. While the CEO shapes the direction, he ensures that every step taken is practical, measured, and results-driven.

Together, they continue to explore new opportunities, expand the company’s footprint, and set new benchmarks in real estate through responsible growth and thoughtful development. With a balance of experience, foresight, and grounded leadership, the Managing Director remains committed to building on the company’s achievements and creating a stronger, more sustainable future.
