COMPANY INTRODUCTION

Shahzad Builders & Developers

01 / 02

Shahzad Builders & Developers is a trusted and progressive real estate development company with a legacy of excellence. Established with the principles of integrity, quality, transparency, and professionalism, this company has grown over the years to become one of Karachi's respected names in the real estate and construction industry.

Our journey began with the vision of our founder, Muhammad Akhtar Qureshi (late), who established M.A. Estate in 1981. Leaving a strong foundation of trust and ethical business processes, and building upon this legacy, Shahzad Builders & Developers, as a subsidiary, has carried forward the commitment to excellence, delivering quality projects and creating lasting value for our clients.

02 / 02

Today, under the leadership of Faizan Qureshi, Chief Executive Officer, Shahzad Builders & Developers continues to build on this proud heritage by embracing innovation, modern construction techniques, and customer-focused development. Our commitment is to create projects that combine superior quality, contemporary design, sustainable development, and long-term value for homeowners and investors.

At Shahzad Builders & Developers, we believe that every project is more than a building - it is a promise of a better future. With a focus on sustainability, energy efficiency, and modern infrastructure, we aim to provide not just homes, but thriving communities and lasting investments.

Our vision is to become one of Pakistan's most trusted and respected real estate organizations. We remain dedicated to shaping vibrant communities, creating landmark developments, and building a legacy that future generations will be proud of.

CEO'S AIM

Building The Future With Purpose

As Chief Executive Officer of Shahzad Builders & Developers, my aim is to build upon our family's legacy by delivering exceptional real estate developments that reflect quality, integrity, and innovation. I am committed to creating projects that exceed customer expectations, contribute to the growth of our communities, and set new benchmarks in Pakistan's real estate industry.

My vision is to transform Shahzad Builders & Developers into one of the country's most trusted and respected real estate organizations through sustainable development, modern construction practices, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. By embracing innovation, maintaining transparency, and placing our clients at the center of every decision, we strive to create lasting value for homeowners, investors, and future generations.

Above all, my aim is to preserve the values established by previous generations while leading the company toward a future defined by growth, trust, professionalism, and enduring success.
