ABOUT US

A legacy built on trust & progress.

Shahzad Builders & Developers is a trusted and progressive real estate development company, built on integrity, quality, transparency and professionalism. Over the years, we have grown into one of Karachi's respected names in the real estate and construction industry.

Building on the foundation established by M.A. Estate in 1981, we carry forward a commitment to exceptional quality, contemporary design and lasting value for homeowners and investors.
